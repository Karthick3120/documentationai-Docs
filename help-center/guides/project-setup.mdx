## Overview

Create and organize your project's documentation in minutes.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    Click "New Project" from your Karthick K dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documentation pages">
    Use the editor to add pages like README, guides, and FAQs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite collaborators">
    Go to Project Settings &gt; Team, then add team members by email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
