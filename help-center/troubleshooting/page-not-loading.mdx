---
title: Page Not Loading
description: Fix when Karthick K pages don't load.
---

## Symptoms

- Blank screen after login
- Docs page stuck loading

## Causes

- Internet connection issues
- Browser cache problems

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    ````bash
    ping karthick.dev
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Reload the page after clearing cache and cookies.
  </Step>
</Steps>
